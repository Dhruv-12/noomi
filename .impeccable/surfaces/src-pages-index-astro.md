# Surface: Noomi pre-launch landing page (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Mobile-first (390px), most traffic from the Instagram in-app browser.
Job: make her want Noomi, explain the two-product philosophy, introduce Calm Serum + Barrier Repair Cream, capture early-access signups (first name + email or WhatsApp). No payment; never "pre-order".
Proof on hand: names, wordmark, palette. Campaign imagery is TEMPORARY generated art direction (src/assets/campaign, provenance embedded), never presented as product photography; no packaging is depicted.
Redesign (2026-09-23): the user rejected the transit/route-line world as too abstract and developer-built. That world is retired.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Two textures. Noomi's two products are two materials, a clear serum and a thick cream, that meet and belong together. The page is a beauty campaign told through liquid, cream and skin in blue light. It refuses the type-only concept page and the category template (white ground, dropper stock, benefit cards, testimonials).

OWN-WORLD: The five palette blues as immersive fields (Mist, Calm, Blue, Depth, Ink), each section blending in from the one above through a tonal band whose dark-to-light stops pass through Blue and Calm (a scroll-driven crossfade was tried and rejected: it put text on the wrong ground mid-transition); a campaign plate hero of a serum drop touching a cream swirl, where only the drop is kept alive by a WebGL liquid shimmer; macro campaign photography of skin, droplets and cream under cool blue light, each temporary image credited "Mood image, not the formula". Archivo variable used at both extremes: condensed black against expanded light. Square edges, no cards, pills or badges.

STORY: Discovery (hero) → why Noomi exists (skincare got loud) → the edit (routine struck down to two) → Calm Serum world → Barrier Repair Cream world → the pair → what she'll see before buying → be first (signup) → final brand moment.

FIRST VIEWPORT (revised 2026-09-24, user: "one clear focal point"): wordmark as a small signature; "Skincare, / edited to two." in the negative space; the 4:5 serum-and-cream plate at full width and native density as the one focal point, with callouts that end on their textures; "Two products, and nothing extra." and a light ghost CTA on the navy. No type over the products. No WebGL.

FORM: Own grounded candidate chosen from three (Blue Hour photo campaign, Two Textures, The Edit shelf), seed key ac5f7d25 (original roll; redesign pinned by user brief). Signature interaction: the serum drop in the hero plate stays liquid (flow and travelling glint) while the plate eases in and zooms with scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
